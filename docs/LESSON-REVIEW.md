# Source lesson review repair

Baseline: public 9ea7c53d21baef394b0b2ca5bce9459819683459. This candidate is private, not deployed.

Reviews have explicit Previous/Next Question, answer fields, Finish review and Retake. A page with several original prompts provides one field per prompt and is kept together because its full original answer slide reveals all answers together. Review navigation is independent of the narration timeline. All previously held recordings remain held; no audio has been regenerated or new timing boundary enabled.

Written answers are not automatically medically graded. After revealing the original source, the learner explicitly marks each entered answer as correct or needing more practice. The result is labeled a self-checked score with all eligible written prompts in its denominator. Blank or unchecked responses earn no point. The sole original multiple-choice item is compared to its original marked option after Reveal. Unanswered recall and provisional ECG interpretations are unscored. No score implies mastery or completes a lesson.

Retake creates a fresh attempt for this lesson, keeps up to 20 previous summaries, and preserves historical lesson completion, audio coverage and independent ECG response records. Within a review, ECG fields use the attempt's own responses rather than deleting previous standalone practice.

Coverage: 22 of 84 source lesson groups contain existing source questions or waveform response activities (259 question pages, 1,283 response fields). The other 62 groups display an explicit absence notice rather than invented questions. Unit 3 Blood Pressure and Progress Check has 8 question pages and 12 written prompts. Other Unit 3 source questions occur in three further groups. No new medical content, question answers or scoring authority was added.

Physical iPad/Safari, actual screen-reader interaction, qualified clinical review and human listening remain unperformed. Contract tests are not rendered-browser qualification.
