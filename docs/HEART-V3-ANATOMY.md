# Original anatomical heart foundation

The default anatomical heart, sections and original-pose comparison use the preserved HRA female v1.2 heart and matching cardiac vasculature. All51 original meshes and208,978triangles remain available in their original shared spatial relationships. The original payload, registered derivative and source inventory are unchanged. No independent abstract cavity is inserted into the displayed heart.

The first anatomical import is recoverable at b9a2531 and the pre-simulator baseline at f5aa0ed06e05ac3388ba240c9bb940d5540f0990. Original model-data.js SHA256 is d1bf8fe79e89aa96b83bad0af9802fa942913be0762d225dee46df64cf29ccab, identical across those versions and the prior live release.

## Geometry and sections

sourced-anatomy.js renders the original surfaces. Four-chamber section fits one oblique display plane through original chamber-region centers; its camera exposes posterior atria and ventricular interiors without moving them. Vascular continuations are hidden for clarity, while papillary and representative valve apparatus retain their positions. Outflow and Anatomical heart restore vascular context. This is an uncapped teaching section, not a validated clinical imaging plane or surgical specimen.

The anterior Section/Wall presets use separate atrial and ventricular display windows; they do not claim one physical slice. The source does not identify measured blood-cavity volumes. Displayed numerical pool volumes are not measurements of atlas geometry. Physical source units remain unknown. The inherited patient basis uses RA-to-LA and IVC-to-SVC landmarks; anterior direction is inferred. Rotation changes screen sides, while anatomy names retain patient sidedness.

## Motion, valves and attachments

One bounded spatial field moves original tissue and matching vessels together. Four chamber-volume coefficients drive illustrative motion; valve/root neighborhoods are pinned and distal influence fades. Shader weights are precomputed; source positions are not rewritten each frame. The renderer has no separate animation clock. Model volumes remain constant in isovolumetric intervals. No calibrated tissue-force or wall-volume-conservation model is claimed.

Original source valves are static and have no supplied animation rig. Original source pose preserves them unchanged. Teaching views use supplementary two-leaflet mitral and three-leaflet tricuspid/semilunar rigs. Fixed insertion contours follow the largest closed source boundary loop in three dimensions, retaining its nonplanar shape. Source-edge coincidence does not identify validated annuli or true commissures. Leaflet shapes, coaptation and opening are representative teaching mechanisms.

Supplementary AV chords join actual leaflet-margin vertices and original source papillary-tip vertices under the shared deformation. They restrain prolapse; they do not open valves. Semilunar rigs have no chordae or papillary support. Selectable attachment rings are supplementary. No separately named interatrial septum exists in the source: selection opens an explicit topology diagram, with no false source leader. Conduction landmarks and microscopic scenes remain representative, not scanned or calibrated enlargements.

## Physiology, terminology and preservation

Pressure, flow, phase, valves and ECG use the existing settled numerical sample. Valve guides require forward flow, an open valve and a forward pressure gradient. Signed venous return is retained. Pulmonary/systemic bed flows differ from semilunar inflow into arterial reservoirs. SVC/IVC and individual pulmonary veins illustrate aggregate edges; branch flow is not calculated. Guides connect source landmarks, not measured lumens. Red/blue flow markers indicate oxygenation convention; exchange occurs at extracardiac schematics. The complete itinerary spans many beats.

Four visible teaching groups are selectable and synchronized: Filling; Atrial contraction; Ventricular contraction; Relaxation. Detailed passive filling, atrial contraction, isovolumetric contraction, ejection and isovolumetric relaxation remain. QRS does not immediately eject blood. Original source pose is explicitly static while numerical readouts retain their selected model time.

Course notes cite 1-based physical pages of All Slideshows Combined (01-13).pdf, including pp112–140,162/167/180 and211–216. Mitral/bicuspid, pulmonary/pulmonic, AV/SL and excitability/irritability vocabulary is preserved with context. Two original quiz items clarify LV wall thickness (p127) and systole versus ejection (p180), preserving question indices and answer keys. Private course PDF/images are not embedded. A full911-page clinical-content certification is not claimed.

Existing equations, all26 microscopic stages, original30heart/20respiratory questions, study modules and saved-progress namespaces remain. Historical authored geometry remains in anatomy.js for existing pure contracts; it no longer supplies the default displayed gross heart.
